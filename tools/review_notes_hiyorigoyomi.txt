2. Purpose and audience
Hiyori Goyomi (ひよりごよみ) is a simple month-view calendar / schedule book for Japanese users who want a calendar without registering an account. Users place events and stamps (e.g. shift marks such as 休 / 早 / 遅, payday, hospital visits, birthdays) on dates. The calendar shows Japanese national holidays (including substitute holidays), rokuyo (大安, 仏滅, etc.) and the old lunar calendar date; these are all calculated on the device, so they are shown without a network connection.

3. How to use (no login or account is needed)
- Launch the app. A short welcome sheet explains that no account is needed; tap "はじめる".
- Tap a date to see that day's events, lunar date and a memo field at the bottom.
- Tap "＋ 予定を追加" to add an event: title, stamp, one of 9 colors, all-day or time, number of days, repeat (daily / weekly / monthly / yearly, every n, end date), reminder and memo.
- Tap "コピー" next to an event, then tap other dates to paste it.
- Tap "スタンプを押す", choose a stamp, then tap dates to place it.
- Search (magnifier icon) finds past and future events and day memos.
- Settings (gear icon): start of week, show/hide rokuyo, themes, color names, default reminder time, backup export/import to a file, read events from the device calendar (read-only), privacy policy.
Basic features (adding, editing, copying, repeating, searching, reminders, backup) are free. There are no ads.

4. External services
- Apple In-App Purchase, managed through RevenueCat (purchase validation and restore). RevenueCat receives only an anonymous app user ID and the App Store transaction.
- No other servers, no login service, no analytics, no ads, no AI services. Events, memos and settings are stored only on the device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. Backup files are written only to a location the user chooses (via the share sheet); the automatic backup (Hiyori Plus) is saved in the app's Documents folder on the device.

5. Regional differences
The app is intended for Japan. The content is in Japanese and holidays and rokuyo follow the Japanese calendar. The app works the same in every region.

6. Third-party material
- The app contains no third-party content or data files. Japanese national holidays are calculated on the device from the rules in the Act on National Holidays (国民の祝日に関する法律); statutes are not subject to copyright in Japan (Copyright Act, Article 13). Rokuyo and the lunar calendar are calculated on the device with published astronomical formulas (J. Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms; main terms of VSOP87). Official data from the Cabinet Office and the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan were used only in our automated tests to verify the calculations and are not included in the app.
- Emoji stamps are drawn with the system emoji font of the device; letter stamps are our own design.
- Open-source libraries (MIT license): Capacitor and its official plugins, @ebarooni/capacitor-calendar, @revenuecat/purchases-capacitor, React.

7. In-App Purchase
One non-consumable product, "ひよりプラス" (Hiyori Plus, product ID hiyori_plus_lifetime), JPY 500, one-time purchase (no subscription). It unlocks all 136 stamps (20 are free), 8 themes (2 are free), naming colors (e.g. "work", "family"), a daily summary notification of today's events, and automatic daily backup (7 days kept).
How to reach it: Calendar > "スタンプを押す" > tap a stamp marked "プラス" (or the "ひよりプラスで、ほかに…種のスタンプが使えます" note). Or Settings (gear icon) > tap the "ひよりプラス" card, or tap a locked theme. The purchase screen shows the free / plus comparison table and the buy button. "以前に買った方はこちら(購入の復元)" (restore) is on the purchase screen.
